Use to calculate the score you would get from playing a hand.

Extra mult and chips would be from any jokers or cards, including the base chips (50) from playing 5 face cards.

## High Card: Steel, Baron and Mime

A king can be red seal and steel at the same time which would mean that with 1 red steel king, you input 1 into red king and 1 into red steel.
