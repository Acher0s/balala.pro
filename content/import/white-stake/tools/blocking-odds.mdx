Blocking a consumable in shop or consumables slots keeps copies from showing up.

The more consumables you block, the higher the chance of getting something you want from rolling or from packs.

Calculations are to determine odds of obtaining one of the wanted consumables with or without blocking.

<Columns>
<Column>

## Tarots

<Calc id="blocking-tarots" title="Tarot blocking">
  <Section title="Pack">
    <Input name="total" label="Total Tarots" default={21} min={1} />
    <Input name="wanted" label="Wanted Tarots" default={5} min={0} />
    <Input name="blocked" label="Tarots Blocked" default={3} min={0} />
    <Input name="inpack" label="Tarots in Pack" default={5} min={1} />
  </Section>
  <Section title="Odds">
    <Output label="No Block" value="1 - combin(total - wanted, inpack) / combin(total, inpack)" format="percent" />
    <Output label="W/Block" value="1 - combin(total - wanted - blocked, inpack) / combin(total - blocked, inpack)" format="percent" />
    <Output label="% Increase" value="(1 - combin(total - wanted - blocked, inpack) / combin(total - blocked, inpack)) / (1 - combin(total - wanted, inpack) / combin(total, inpack)) - 1" format="percent" />
  </Section>
</Calc>

</Column>
