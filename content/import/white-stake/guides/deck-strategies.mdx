## Red Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="red" card />
</Center>

4 hands, 4 discards and 8 handsize, you can see 43 cards without any deck fixing<br />
Allows you to be greedier when playing down for value within the deck such as seals and rebate

## Blue Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="blue" card />
</Center>

Same as Red deck with the benefit of having more synergies from econ jokers that work by playing hands as well as gold seals and lucky cards benefiting from being used more

## Yellow Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="yellow" card />
</Center>

This deck allows you to purchase more at the start, whether it be a pack for an enhancement or hermit, as well as being able to purchase early card packs to have something of value to death (seals/glass/poly)

## Green Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="green" card />
</Center>

Money is obtained from hands and discard rather than interest so a scoring joker that gets you out faster can be useful to serve as econ<br />
Saving money is also important as arcana packs can have hermits which gets you much further in shops

## Black Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="black" card />
</Center>

With 3 hands to be played, it is more important to have a few good scoring jokers to reduce the chances of dying to a blind<br />
It is also important to remember that there is 6 joker slots which can often be forgotten as every other deck has less

## Magic Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="magic" card />
</Center>

It is important to save enough money to buy arcana packs early as getting a devil, hermit, or magician and using both fools on them will result in having a strong start to the game

## Nebula Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="nebula" card />
</Center>

With 1 less consumable slots, having more gold seals and other retriggerable cards matters more as you can only get the value of 1 blue seal or a few purples should that be your copy target

## Ghost Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="ghost" card />
</Center>

Finding glass through a spectral or from packs is important as you want to quickly get dejavu and make a very strong, glass red seal copy target

## Abandoned Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="abandoned" card />
</Center>

1 hanged man guarantees draws when discarding or playing 5 cards at a time<br />
Opening a lot of card packs is useful here as it is much easier to draw your whole deck

## Checkered Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="checkered" card />
</Center>

Much easier to unlock flush house as it only requires 1 extra card of either suit to unlock, can be greedier in blind with a few flush levels as you are very likely to be able to draw one<br />
In the first pvp, if you do not have any scoring jokers or useful tarots, then the difference maker can be going for a straight flush over playing multiple flushes

## Zodiac Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="zodiac" card />
</Center>

With this deck, econ is even more important as you will be able to find many more planets and tarots simply from rolling<br />
Furthermore, you can go for a lot stronger of a final joker lineup since overstock allows you to see 50% more slots with each roll

## Painted Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="painted" card />
</Center>

This deck is unique as it offers more support for tempo playstyle and high card<br />
Since there's only 4 jokers, quickly taking your opponent's lives and forcing them to sell out of econ quickly allows you to build up more in the late game when more econ options are available<br />
The extra 2 hand size lets you hold more gold cards, blue seals, and steel cards making high card more relevant as you can gain more value

## Anaglyph Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="anaglyph" card />
</Center>

Important to consider the tags that are relevant to you when skipping<br />
Best ones are usually money based (handy, economy, investment)<br />
or joker related (Buffoon, Rare)<br />
To increase variance, ethereal or negative can be used<br />
For high card or heavy steel usage, a juggle tag can be considered for a quick pvp win<br />
Others are rarely used but can be considered if it will likely benefit you more than missing a shop or a stronger skip tag later on

## Plasma Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="plasma" card />
</Center>

Chips are strong in the early game but once it gets past a certain point, xmult becomes better<br />
You can determine which is better by quickly calculating the xmult with your best hand and see if it would pass the chips in your best hand, generally a good pivot point is when you find bloodstone, idol, ancient, or other combination that is around 10x

## Erratic Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="erratic" card />
</Center>

Since most erratic decks have a bunch of one/more cards, it becomes very easy to unlock 5oak which can then be used to 1 shot every blind until pvp, allowing you to save up to $25 interest quickly

## Violet Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="mp_violet" card />
</Center>

Generally you want to pick up vouchers that are useful as soon as possible since the second tier is likely to showup, or missing a voucher could result in a stronger voucher being missed since the tier 1 or 2 of a different voucher could show up again

## Orange Deck

<Center>
  <Deck id="mp_orange" card />
</Center>

This deck generally results in a seal of some sorts or a glass card, this is likely to become your death target due to how early it was obtained and can easily snowball if copied frequently
