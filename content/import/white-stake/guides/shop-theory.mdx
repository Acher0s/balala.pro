## Reroll Theory

<Center>
  <Joker id="chaos" card />
</Center>

Spreading out your rolling helps keep the average cost low<br />
Generally you want to roll a few times but also be able to exit blinds at the $25 mark for full interest<br />
It is also important to consider whether you are going to sell your econ (to use invis or prepare for final pvp) and avoid spending too much in the shop prior to this to roll into other econ or keep the average cost low

## Pack Order

Generally you want to roll a few times to block tarots in shop then open an arcana pack with around $20 or good tarots to use<br />
Blocking planets before opening planet packs are ideal as well<br />
With spectral packs, you want to play for either an ankh or wraith, meaning that with a blueprint, you may want to open the pack to potentially ankh it first then roll to find econ or scoring

## Voucher Order

<Center>
  <Voucher id="crystal_ball" card />
</Center>

If a voucher provides immediate benefit that is good for you (such as overstock or clearance sale), buying it first may be better depending on if you have enough econ to get back to interest in the blind<br />
Clearance can be taken before opening an arcana pack if you are above $20 afterwards in case there's a hermit, or afterwards if you gain enough money to stay above the interest cap from the arcana pack<br />
With few blue/purple seals or econ to sustain multiple tarots/econ, vouchers such as crystal ball and seed money can wait until right before pvp

## Tarot Theory

<Tool id="shop-rolls" />

<Center>
  <Voucher id="tarot_tycoon" card />
</Center>

Taking Tarot Merchant decreases jokers by 17% while doubling tarots<br />
Taking Tarot Tycoon decreases jokers by 50% and quadrupling tarots<br />
Difference between Merchant to Tycoon is -40% jokers, 2x tarots<br />
Tycoon is best taken when holding several S tier jokers with a strong need for deck fixing (idol/ancient/baron mime) and/or making a lot of money ($100+)
