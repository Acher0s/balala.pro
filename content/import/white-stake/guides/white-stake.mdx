By King with help from PPTT chatters and BMP Team

This document aims to explain the thought processes and calculations used by high ranking players to win ranked games

**Aims to:**

- Explain methods on maximizing econ, value, and scoring
- Explain the various playstyles used in games
- Explain the value of each part of the game

**Does NOT:**

- Guarantee mmr gains
- Go into detail on multiplayer changes or winning single player runs

<Callout title="Disclaimers">

- This document focuses on the ranked ruleset
- A lot of this was written at night/during ptt games and may not be 100% perfect
- Values of certain things can be different based on playstyles and scenarios

</Callout>
