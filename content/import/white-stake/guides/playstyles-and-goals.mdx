## Econ/value

<Center>
  <Joker id="mail" card />
</Center>

More $ = more scoring, better deck fixing, and stronger econ/value<br />
Earlier suit changer/planet levels can make up the difference of a 2-4x joker while providing better deck fixing and scoring potential for late game<br />
Finding stronger value generators earlier helps prepare for S tier jokers faster<br />
To maximize value, you want to keep the least number of scoring jokers to get as close to the score as possible and use every discard/hand (if it makes sense) to gain all the value in your deck (seals, enhancements, etc.)

</Level>

<Level of="intermediate">

## Tempo

<Center>
  <Joker id="campfire" card />
</Center>

Quickly taking your opponent's lives to win faster or pressure them to sell out of value generators to survive<br />
Tempo comes from jokers, tarots, or even a holo/glass card<br />
A tarot can provide flush house or other strong hand that strongly synergizes with your joker lineup<br />
An extra scoring joker over econ can lead to more breathing room to later utilize stronger econ/value generators<br />
In white stake, it is generally best to keep your value generators and only do a quick switch on a few jokers heading into pvp so that you are not too far behind your opponent in shops leading them to find a strong joker (ancient/idol) much earlier than you and then proceeding to lose every pvp after

</Level>

<Level of="beginner">

## Survival

<Center>
  <Joker id="smiley" card />
</Center>

If you are struggling or mathematically unable to live blinds, then rolling a few times to increase chances of getting a tarot/joker/planet to survive may be best<br />
A good way to determine if you can survive blinds is by rounding your mult from jokers and planets,<br />
then multiplying it by the chips from jokers, planets and cards<br />
Example: 25 mult smiley x (50 chips sly +50 chips facecards) = 25\*100 = 2500

</Level>

## Accessing Logs

How to access logs:

For Windows: Press Windows + R keys > type `%appdata%/balatro/mods/lovely/logs`

For mac: Press Command + Shift + G > then type `/Users/[User's Name (replace this with your user)]/Library/Application Support/Balatro/Mods/lovely/logs`

After locating your logs you can grab your latest log (make sure to sort by date) and upload it into the Log Parser on [https://balatromp.com/log-parser](https://balatromp.com/log-parser)
