## Mime Copy

<Center>
  <Joker id="mime" card />
</Center>

To maximize mime value, you can play your final hand then quickly switch your copy joker (bp/br) to mime to also get the full value of mime before the blind/pvp ends

## Collection

<Center>
  <Joker id="ancient" card />
</Center>

Before spending money in shop and rolling before an important pvp, you can check collections to see what idol or ancient hit and can get the relevant tarots in case you find either in shop

## Hone

<Center>
  <Voucher id="hone" card />
</Center>

This works immediately so you can purchase hone then open a card pack to increase the chances of obtaining a poly card

## PVP Bloodstone

<Center>
  <Joker id="bloodstone" card />
</Center>

Bloodstone has the same number of hits in a pvp, meaning that if bloodstone hit 3 times on an earlier flush, then it will hit the same number of times on your final hand (with the same number of triggers), so you can copy bloodstone with blueprint earlier in a pvp then figure out if it is worth copying over a photo on your stronger hand

## Buffering

<Center>
  <Joker id="odd_todd" card />
</Center>

In pvps you can play your hand and then press esc, the deck, or one of the buttons on the left so that your hand goes through (with luckies or other value) which allows you to hide your final score while also gaining more value from playing an extra hand

## Weaker Hand?

<Center>
  <Joker id="ice_cream" card />
</Center>

If your opponent has used all their hands and you have calculated a score that will beat theirs, you can play a weaker hand that barely beats theirs so that they are less likely to know that you have a much stronger joker lineup than theirs which would otherwise cause them to sell many of their jokers to go full econ and find what they are missing

## Gambling

<Center>
  <Joker id="mp_lets_go_gambling" card />
</Center>

If your opponent has to play their hands first due to turn order, you can find out how many times it goes "oops" and gives you money, which lets you sell out of gambling to avoid giving them money<br />
You can also guess to when it gives a 4x based on a jump in score letting you play your best hand to maximize your score

## Card Packs

When you open card packs, the cards you pick up are sent to the bottom of your deck so it will not showup in other packs for that blind unless you can see every card

## Loyalty Card

<Center>
  <Joker id="loyalty_card" card />
</Center>

Lining up loyalty means having it occur on your last hand in pvp<br />
This allows you to dig deeper into your deck while also allowing for synergies from acrobat and dusk should you have or find them before entering pvp

### Lining it up

<Center>
  <Voucher id="nacho_tong" card />
</Center>

With 4 hands, you want your last hand in pvp to be loyalty proc, then you want to play every hand leading up to the next pvp to use it on the final hand again<br />
With 6 hands, you want your last hand of any blind or pvp to be your loyalty hand<br />
With 5 hands, you want to exit big blind with loyalty saying 4 hands remaining in shop<br />
or exit small blind with loyalty saying 3 hands remaining in shop<br />
or exit pvp with it saying 2 hands remaining
