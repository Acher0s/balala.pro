Drop in a Lovely log from a game played on the 0.5.x Multiplayer mod (up to 0.5.5) to see every multiplayer game in it: your jokers and vouchers, what you spent in the shops, the PvP blinds, both decks and The Idol's rolls.

Your logs are in `%AppData%\Balatro\Mods\lovely\log` on Windows. Your files are read in your browser and never uploaded.
